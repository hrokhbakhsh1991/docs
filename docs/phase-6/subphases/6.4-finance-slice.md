### Handler flow (TourCreated → ledger outbox)

1. `createDenaliFinanceOutboxConsumer` reads pending rows via `OutboxReader`.
2. For each unseen `domainEventId`, `handleTourCreatedLedgerEvent` runs when `eventType === "TourCreated"` and payload carries `registrationId` + `paidAmountMinor`.
3. `postDoubleEntryJournal` materializes a balanced debit/credit pair; `emitFinanceLedgerDoubleEntryAppliedOutbox` enqueues `finance.ledger.double_entry_applied` through `OutboxWriter`.
4. Replay of the same inbound `domainEventId` is a no-op (in-memory idempotency store inside the consumer until Phase 5.4 wires persistent dedupe).
