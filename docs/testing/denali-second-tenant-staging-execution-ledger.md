## Confirmed design constraints

- Keep the same staging applications and PostgreSQL database; do not reuse tenant A's ID, hosts, or identity rows for B.
- No global development/static tenant mapping for `denali-qa2`; the host-to-tenant binding must be explicit, auditable and consistent across API, web, portal and edge.
- Do not activate a hostname until real DNS/edge reachability and TLS have been proven.
- Use the app database role for business-path proofs. The database superuser is reserved for controlled provisioning after dry-run/allowlist checks.
- Keep B's external integrations disabled; synthetic `offline_receipt` only. No payment gateway or real outbound notification is part of the plan.
- Never infer staging success from local tests, a healthy endpoint, source inspection, or historical ledger rows.
